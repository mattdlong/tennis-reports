### No-Vig Calculation Method

No-vig probabilities calculated using:
```
P_no_vig = (1 / decimal_odds) / sum(1 / all_odds)
```

For totals (2.19 / 1.72):
- P(Over) = (1/2.19) / (1/2.19 + 1/1.72) = 44.0%
- P(Under) = (1/1.72) / (1/2.19 + 1/1.72) = 56.0%
