```
No-Vig Probability = (1 / Decimal Odds) / [(1 / Odds_A) + (1 / Odds_B)]
```

**Example (Spread):**
- Raw implied: Cerundolo 80.6% (1.24 odds), Nava 27.4% (3.65 odds)
- Vig: 8.0%
- No-vig: Cerundolo 74.6%, Nava 25.4%
- Model: Cerundolo 93% cover probability
- **Edge:** 93% - 74.6% = +18.4 pp
